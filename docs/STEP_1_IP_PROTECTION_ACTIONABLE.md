## ✅ STEP 1A: MEXICO COPYRIGHT REGISTRATION (Week 1)

### **What You'll Do:**
Register your copyright with INDAUTOR (Mexican copyright office)

### **How to Do It:**

**Option 1: Online (Recommended - 10 minutes)**

1. **Go to website:**
   ```
   https://www.gob.mx/indautor
   ```

2. **Click "Registro de Obras"** (Register Works)

3. **Create account:**
   - Email address
   - Password
   - Confirm email

4. **Start registration:**
   - Click "Solicitar registro"
   - Select: "Obra de software" (software work)

5. **Fill form:**
   - Title: "EV2 Clandestinoz Nightclub Management System"
   - Author: Your name
   - Description: "Complete nightclub management platform with 7 integrated systems"
   - Creation date: Today
   - Language: English (or Spanish)

6. **Upload files:**
   - Main file: Source code (ZIP all backend files)
   - Description: Include all 9 server files
   - Format: ZIP or RAR

7. **Review & Pay:**
   - Fee: ~$650 MXN (~$40 USD)
   - Payment method: Online (credit card, bank transfer)

8. **Submit:**
   - Review all info
   - Click "Enviar solicitud"
   - You'll get certificate in 2-3 weeks

### **Option 2: In-Person (If preferred)**

1. **Visit INDAUTOR office:**
   - Address: Nogales has IMPI office (INDAUTOR handles copyrights)
   - Bring: ID, proof of address, printed form

2. **Submit documents:**
   - Application form (fill out there)
   - Copy of source code
   - Payment receipt

3. **Get receipt:**
   - They give you reference number
   - Certificate arrives by mail in 2-3 weeks

## ✅ STEP 1B: MEXICO TRADEMARK REGISTRATION (Week 1-2)

### **What You'll Do:**
Register your brand name & logo with IMPI (Mexican IP Institute)

### **How to Do It:**

**Online Registration:**

1. **Go to website:**
   ```
   https://www.gob.mx/impi
   ```

2. **Select "Trámites" (Procedures)**

3. **Choose "Registro de Marca"** (Trademark Registration)

4. **Create account or login:**
   - Email
   - Password
   - RFC (Mexican tax ID) - if you have one

5. **Fill application:**
   ```
   - Mark Type: Word/Logo combined
   - Description: "EV2 Clandestinoz nightclub management brand"
   - Classes: 
     * Class 42: Software services
     * Class 35: Business services
     * Class 36: Financial services (for payments)
   ```

6. **Upload logo:**
   - Your EV2 Clandestinoz logo (PNG, JPG)
   - Size: At least 500x500px
   - Clear, professional image

7. **Pay fees:**
   - Basic registration: ~$2,500 MXN (~$145 USD)
   - Per additional class: ~$500 MXN each
   - Total for 3 classes: ~$3,500 MXN (~$200 USD)

8. **Submit & Wait:**
   - Receive confirmation email
   - Office examines for 1-2 months
   - You get approval certificate
   - Registration valid for 10 years

## ✅ STEP 1D: USA TRADEMARK REGISTRATION (Week 2)

### **What You'll Do:**
Register trademark with USPTO (US Patent & Trademark Office)

### **How to Do It:**

**Online:**

1. **Go to website:**
   ```
   https://www.uspto.gov
   ```

2. **Click "Trademark Electronic Application System"** (TEAS)

3. **Select filing basis:**
   - Click "TEAS Plus" (cheaper: $250 vs $350)
   - OR "TEAS Standard" ($350)

4. **Start application:**
   - Trademark: "EV2 Clandestinoz"
   - Type: Combination of words and logo (if you have logo)

5. **Describe goods/services:**
   ```
   - Class 42: Software development services
   - Class 35: Business and administrative services
   - Class 36: Financial and payment services
   - Specific description: "Cloud-based nightclub management software; Employee payment processing; Point of sale system integration"
   ```

6. **Upload logo:**
   - PNG or JPEG format
   - 300x300 pixels minimum
   - High quality, professional

7. **Basis for filing:**
   - Select: "Use in commerce" (you'll use it in business)
   - Date of first use: Today or when you'll start using it
   - Date of first use in commerce: Same

8. **Owner information:**
   - Your name
   - Address
   - Email
   - Phone

9. **Payment:**
   - $250 for TEAS Plus
   - $350 for TEAS Standard
   - Credit card
   - Pays for 1 class; add more classes for extra $100 each

10. **Review & Sign:**
    - Review all info carefully
    - Check "I own this trademark"
    - E-sign with password
    - Submit

11. **After submission:**
    - Get confirmation number (save it!)
    - Trademark Office examines in 2-3 months
    - You'll get email updates
    - If approved, certificate arrives
    - Registration good for 10 years
