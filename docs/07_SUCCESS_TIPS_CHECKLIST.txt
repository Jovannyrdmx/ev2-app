## ✅ DO'S - BEST PRACTICES

### Before You Send Emails
✅ Research investor thoroughly (their portfolio, what they invest in)  
✅ Find a warm introduction (don't cold email)  
✅ Personalize every email (use investor's name, reference their investments)  
✅ Test PDF attachment (make sure it opens properly)  
✅ Spell-check everything (no typos, ever)  
✅ Proofread email subject line  
✅ Have all contact info ready (your phone, email, LinkedIn)  

### Email Best Practices
✅ Keep subject line under 50 characters  
✅ Keep body to 3 paragraphs max  
✅ Use short sentences (easier to read)  
✅ Include clear call-to-action (ask for 20-minute call)  
✅ Include your contact info in signature  
✅ Attach PDF (not PowerPoint)  
✅ Send from professional email  
✅ Follow up after 48 hours if no response  

### Meeting Best Practices
✅ Arrive 15 minutes early  
✅ Dress professionally  
✅ Make eye contact  
✅ Tell a compelling story  
✅ Use visuals (show product demo)  
✅ Listen more than you talk  
✅ Address concerns honestly  
✅ Ask great questions about their portfolio/focus  
✅ Take notes  
✅ Confirm next steps before leaving  

### Follow-Up Best Practices
✅ Send thank you email within 24 hours  
✅ Reference specific things they mentioned  
✅ Share relevant documents they asked about  
✅ Follow up weekly with updates  
✅ Move toward term sheet quickly  
✅ Keep momentum going  
✅ Be persistent but not pushy

## ❌ DON'Ts - AVOID THESE MISTAKES

### Email Mistakes
❌ Don't send cold emails (always get warm intro)  
❌ Don't use generic templates (personalize each email)  
❌ Don't make email too long (keep it short)  
❌ Don't use multiple different fonts/colors  
❌ Don't attach huge files (keep PDFs <5MB)  
❌ Don't send after 9pm or before 8am  
❌ Don't use unprofessional email address  
❌ Don't misspell investor's name (check 3x)  
❌ Don't oversell your product (let data speak)  
❌ Don't wait more than 48 hours to follow up  

### Meeting Mistakes
❌ Don't arrive late (always early)  
❌ Don't dress casually (always professional)  
❌ Don't talk too much (listen more)  
❌ Don't bring unprepared materials  
❌ Don't show broken demo (test everything first)  
❌ Don't make promises you can't keep  
❌ Don't criticize competitors harshly  
❌ Don't avoid tough questions (address them)  
❌ Don't forget to ask for the investment  
❌ Don't forget their contact info after meeting  

### Pitch Mistakes
❌ Don't focus on features (focus on benefits)  
❌ Don't use too many numbers (2-3 key metrics)  
❌ Don't make it about you (make it about them)  
❌ Don't be too rehearsed (sound natural)  
❌ Don't ramble (clear, concise points)  
❌ Don't apologize for anything  
❌ Don't show lack of confidence  
❌ Don't discuss politics or controversial topics  
❌ Don't criticize their previous investments  
❌ Don't forget your elevator pitch
