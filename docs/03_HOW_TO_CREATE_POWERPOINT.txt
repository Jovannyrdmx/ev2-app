# HOW TO CREATE POWERPOINT IN 30 MINUTES
**Simple Step-by-Step Guide**

---

## WHAT YOU NEED

1. Microsoft PowerPoint (or Google Slides - free)
2. The file: `EV2_INVESTOR_PRESENTATION.pptx.md` (on your Desktop)
3. Your company logo (optional)
4. 15 minutes of free time

---

## STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

### STEP 1: Open PowerPoint (2 minutes)
- Open Microsoft PowerPoint
- Click "Blank Presentation"
- Delete the default slide

### STEP 2: Set Design Template (3 minutes)
- Go to Design tab
- Choose a professional template (recommended: "Ion" or "Dividend")
- Color scheme: Dark blue background, white text, light accent color
- Apply to all slides

### STEP 3: Create Slides - Copy & Paste (15 minutes)

- Title: "EV2"

**Slide 3: Solution**

  - Unified management

## PRESENTATION TIPS

### Before You Present
- Practice 20+ times (out loud!)
- Time yourself (aim for 45 min)
- Test your internet/laptop
- Arrive 15 minutes early
- Have backup battery

### During Presentation
- Make eye contact
- Tell a story (problem → solution → traction)
- Use visuals, not text
- Pause for questions
- Show passion & excitement
- Be ready to go off-script

### After Presentation
- Send thank you email (24 hours)
- Share supporting documents
- Follow up weekly
- Move toward term sheet
