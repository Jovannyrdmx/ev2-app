## 💡 HOW TIPS WORK

1. **Guest tips through main app**
2. **Tip goes to employee account**
3. **Employee logs in to portal**
4. **Sees tip in dashboard**
5. **Can request withdrawal**
6. **Money sent to their bank**

---

## 🎵 SONG REQUEST TIPS

1. **Guest requests song & tips DJ**
2. **DJ gets tip in their account**
3. **DJ sees it in "Earnings" tab**
4. **Counted separately from regular tips**
5. **Can withdraw with other earnings**

## 💸 WITHDRAWAL PROCESS

1. Employee goes to "💰 Earnings" tab
2. Enters amount to withdraw
3. Clicks "💰 Request Withdrawal"
4. System verifies:
   - Sufficient earnings
   - Payment method verified
   - Bank account valid
5. Withdrawal processed
6. Funds sent in 2-3 business days
